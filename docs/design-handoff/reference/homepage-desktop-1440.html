Approach

Directed first. Generated second.

The tools change every month. The order of work does not.

  1. 01

    Direction

    A written treatment and shot list before anything is generated.

  2. 02

    Visual development

    Look frames, characters and light, locked before any motion.

  3. 03

    Editing

    Cut for rhythm first, then for story, then for length.

  4. 04

    Sound

    Score, sound design and mix built to the picture, never added last.

  5. 05

    Finishing

    Upscale, grade and delivery masters for every placement.
