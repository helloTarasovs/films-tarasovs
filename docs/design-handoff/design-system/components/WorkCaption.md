# WorkCaption
Index, title, category line, tag and the "Watch film" action, on a hairline under the frame.

- Index `caption` mono in `muted-foreground`; title `h3` (Cormorant 600, 28–32px); category `body-s` in `fg-secondary` with ratio and runtime.
- Tag: `Commissioned` (plum dot) or `Self-initiated` (no dot). The word carries the meaning; the dot is a secondary cue.
- Hover on the frame or link turns the title `primary-text`.
- Compact variant for verticals and mobile: action moves under the title.
